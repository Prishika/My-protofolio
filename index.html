About Me

Who Am I?

Hi, I'm Rishika — a PhD researcher in Biomedical Engineering at the University of Houston, with a background in computer science, data analytics, and applied artificial intelligence.

My research focuses on biomarker discovery for triple-negative breast cancer using high-dimensional biomedical data and machine learning. I work with Python, SQL, support vector machines, and XGBoost to explore patterns that may support future early-detection research.

Alongside my research, I have built semantic search, text classification, RAG, and LLM workflows, and developed dashboards and automated data pipelines for enterprise analytics. I enjoy connecting research questions with practical, carefully evaluated technical solutions.

PhD Research

What questions drive my research?

ONGOING · PHD RESEARCH

AI-based biomarker identification for triple-negative breast cancer

I study high-dimensional biomedical data to identify genomic and proteomic biomarker candidates for TNBC. My research combines careful data preparation, feature selection, and supervised machine learning.

The goal is to support future early-detection research and therapeutic target discovery.

View Google Scholar ↗
01

Prepare biomedical data

Normalization, missing-value imputation, and class-imbalance handling.

02

Identify informative features

Chi-square, ANOVA F-test, recursive feature elimination, and LASSO regularization.

03

Build and compare models

Support vector machines with linear and RBF kernels, alongside XGBoost.

04

Explore feature structure

PCA and t-SNE to visualize patterns in high-dimensional datasets.
